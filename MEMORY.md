# MEMORY.md — Long-Term Memory

_This file is your agent's long-term memory. It is loaded only in your main session (direct chats with your human). Keep it personal, structured, and up to date._

## How to use

- **Daily notes** live in `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (raw logs of what happened).
- **This file** is the curated essence: decisions, context, lessons, people, preferences.
- Review daily notes periodically and fold what matters into this file.
- Never store secrets here (tokens, passwords).

## Suggested structure

```markdown
# MEMORY.md

## About my human
- Name:
- What to call them:
- Pronouns:
- Timezone:
- Notes:

## Projects
- Project name — status, goals, key decisions

## Preferences
- How my human likes to work, communicate, decide

## Lessons learned
- Mistakes not to repeat, rules that emerged

## Key decisions
- Date — decision — why

## People
- Who matters, roles, how to reach them
```

_Start small. Add entries as you go — this file grows with your relationship._
