# IDEAS.md — Idea Backlog

A place to collect ideas for your own Futuremen instance. The original project keeps its private backlog; this file is intentionally blank so you can make it yours.

## How to use

- Add ideas as dated entries, most recent on top.
- Keep them short: concept, why it matters, rough scope.
- When an idea becomes a real plan, move it to your calendar/roadmap and mark it here.
